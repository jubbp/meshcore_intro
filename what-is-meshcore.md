# What Is MeshCore?

MeshCore is a way for nearby devices to relay messages across a local mesh network.

In practical terms, this means:

- Local communication that does not depend on one central tower
- Better resilience during outages and congestion
- Community ownership of local connectivity

## Why people use it

- Emergency preparedness
- Neighborhood coordination
- Learning and experimentation with local networking
