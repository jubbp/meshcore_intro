# Get Connected

If you are new, follow these steps:

1. Learn the basics on the [What is MeshCore?]({{ '/what-is-meshcore/' | relative_url }}) page.
2. Contact the Bunbury team through the [Contact Us]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) page.
3. Ask for beginner setup guidance and recommended local options.

## Beginner checklist

- Share your suburb or general area (not exact address)
- Share your goal (learning, emergency prep, community)
- Ask what hardware/software is currently recommended locally
